Trade Like the Greats started with a simple frustration. You can read Minervini's books. You can watch Camillo's interviews. You can study Graham's original texts. But reading about a methodology and being able to apply it to a live chart are two completely different things.

The gap between knowing a framework and thinking in it is enormous. It takes years of repetition, of asking the right questions, of running setup after setup through the same mental model until it becomes instinct. Most traders never get there.

The platform exists to close that gap. To let you run any stock through Minervini's methodology and see exactly what he would be looking at, what would concern him, what would excite him — before you've spent a decade internalizing his rules yourself.

What this is

Trade Like the Greats is an educational platform. Everything here is methodology analysis — not investment advice, not buy signals, not a financial newsletter. The AI engines apply publicly documented frameworks to help you think through setups more rigorously.

The goal is to make you a better thinker about markets, not to think for you.

The principles behind the build

Methodology over tips

We teach how great traders think, not what to buy. Understanding the reasoning is worth infinitely more than any single pick.

Unlimited queries

We don't cap how much you can learn. Artificial query limits are a tax on curiosity. Every paid tier is unlimited.

Honest disclaimers

We are an educational platform. We will never pretend otherwise. All content is methodology analysis, not personalized investment advice.

Serious traders only

This platform is not for people looking for shortcuts. It is for people who want to do the work of learning how the greats actually think.

The name question

The platform was previously called Trader Atlas. We renamed it because Trade Like the Greats is more honest about what it actually does — it is not a directory of traders, it is a tool for learning to think like them. If you are wondering about the trademark status, we recommend checking the USPTO database at tmsearch.uspto.gov and consulting a trademark attorney before using the name commercially.
